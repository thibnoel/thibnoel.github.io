## Problem

Self-collision detection and avoidance are essential for reactive control of dynamic robots with legs or arms.
Few control methods handle these constraints directly, so collision-free trajectories are often left to a
planner upstream of the controller.

## Approach

Two lightweight, conservative and smooth models are combined to handle self-collisions generically:

- **Distant body pairs** (e.g. segments of distinct legs): forward kinematics on simplified geometries,
  with analytical derivatives.
- **Body pairs moving close to one another**: a learned approximation of the collision distance in joint space.

## Results

The models were integrated in an on-board safety controller, deployed in real time on the SOLO 12 robot, and
published at ICRA 2021.
