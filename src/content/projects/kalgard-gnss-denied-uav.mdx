## Context

Kalgard Systems is an early-stage startup, incubated at the Inria Startup Studio, developing a localisation
module that lets UAVs navigate when GNSS is unavailable.

## Contribution

- **Software architecture**: a ROS 2 localisation stack split into ROS-free computational cores, explicit data
  contracts and thin ROS adapters.
- **Data tooling**: a georeferenced data pipeline combining Sentinel-2 imagery, IGN data and OpenStreetMap.
- **Mechanical design**: OnShape design of the second-generation module prototype.
