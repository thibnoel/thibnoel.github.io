## Context

The [H2020 Pro-Act project](https://www.h2020-pro-act.eu/) developed an analogue demonstration system for
collaborative manipulation on the Moon, with the long-term objective of in-situ resource utilisation (ISRU).
The consortium included GMV, DFKI, PIAP, Thales UK, AVS, LPRC, City University of London and LAAS-CNRS.

## Contribution

- Modelling and simulation of the project's environment and robots: robot models, sensors and lunar terrain.
- Deployment of the simulation on a dedicated AWS cloud server.
