## Context

The [H2020 Pro-Act project](https://www.h2020-pro-act.eu/) developed an analogue demonstration system for
collaborative manipulation on the Moon, with the long-term objective of in-situ resource utilisation (ISRU).

## Contribution

Development of the project's simulation environments, including robot models, sensors and lunar terrain models.
